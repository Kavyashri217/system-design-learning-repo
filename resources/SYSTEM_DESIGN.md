# Free System Design Resources

A curated list of free system design resources on the Internet: videos, articles, repos, courses, and reading to help you learn and practice system design.

## YouTube channels & playlists

- Gaurav Sen — System design, scalable systems, and interview walkthroughs.
  https://www.youtube.com/@GauravSensei
- Back To Back SWE — Focused system design and algorithm interviews with diagrams and step-by-step solutions.
  https://www.youtube.com/c/BackToBackSWE

## GitHub repositories

- System Design Primer — A comprehensive guide to system design interviews and reading list.
  https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer
- Awesome Scalability — Curated list of resources on scalability, performance, and system design topics.
  https://github.com/binhnguyennus/awesome-scalability

## Articles & blogs

- High Scalability — Real-world architecture posts and breakdowns of large-scale systems.
  http://highscalability.com/
- Martin Kleppmann — Blog and articles on distributed data systems (author of "Designing Data-Intensive Applications").
  https://martin.kleppmann.com/

## Courses & lectures (free)

- MIT 6.824: Distributed Systems — Lecture notes, labs, and videos (excellent for practical distributed systems concepts).
  https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.824/
- Stanford / other university lecture videos — search for "distributed systems lecture" or specific course codes on YouTube and university pages.

## Papers & reading

- Read classic papers (CAP theorem, Paxos/Raft papers). Many are freely available online; search by title (e.g., "Paxos Made Simple", "Raft consensus algorithm").
- Data-Intensive Systems reading: companion materials and blog posts by Martin Kleppmann.
  https://dataintensive.net/

## Practice & mock interviews

- Recreate system design problems from the System Design Primer and design them on a whiteboard or Google Docs.
- Watch video walkthroughs (Gaurav Sen, Back To Back SWE) and re-draw the architecture diagrams yourself.

## Tools & diagrams

- Draw.io (diagrams.net) — free diagramming tool for architecture diagrams.
  https://app.diagrams.net/
- C4 model resources — helpful for structuring system views (context, containers, components).
  https://c4model.com/
